Padel Score: Pad & Voice Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026 · Padel Score: Pad & Voice (in Arabic, حاسبة البادل), called Padel Score below, for iPhone, Apple Watch and iPad

In short: Padel Score doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app has no servers. Everything you enter, and any Health data it uses, stays on your own devices.

What the app stores, and where

  • Matches and players. Player names you type in, scores and your match history are saved on your iPhone and Apple Watch. They move directly between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch, using Apple's built-in watch connection, so both can keep score.
  • iPad scoreboard. To show the live score on an iPad, your iPhone connects directly to the iPad nearby over Bluetooth. Nothing goes through the internet. An iPad only receives scores from iPhones you allow on that iPad.
  • Settings. Your choices, like language, appearance and voice, are saved on each device.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Padel Score uses Apple Health on your Apple Watch during a match:

  • It records the match as a workout and saves it to Health.
  • It reads your heart rate and active calories while you play, to show them on the watch.

Health data never leaves your devices through Padel Score. It isn't sent to any server, shared with anyone, sold, used for advertising or stored in iCloud by the app. You can change or remove access at any time in the Health app or in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Padel Score.

Other permissions

  • Notifications: created on your iPhone itself, for example to tell you the watch app is open. They aren't sent from any server.
  • Bluetooth: used only to connect your iPhone to your iPad scoreboard, including while your iPhone is locked during a match.

Spoken score

The iPad can call the score out loud. It uses Apple's voices on the iPad, or recordings built into the app. The app doesn't record or listen to you.

Third parties

Padel Score contains no third-party code for analytics, advertising or tracking, and shares no data with anyone.

Your control

  • Delete matches any time in the app's History.
  • Turn off Health, notification or local network access in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from that device.

Children

Padel Score is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
